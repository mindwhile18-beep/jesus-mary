Who We Are

About Our Institution

"Knowledge is Power"

At Infant Jesus English Medium High School, we live by our motto. Established with a vision to provide quality English medium education to the children of Vinjamur and surrounding regions, we make high-standard education accessible, engaging, and stress-free.

We are recognized by the Government of Andhra Pradesh and run by the dedicated Joseph's Foundation Charitable Trust. Under their guidance, we aim to spread love and kindness while shaping morally upright, intellectually outstanding citizens.
