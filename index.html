About Our School

Empowering Through Education

Keeping view of our vision, goals, and objectives, J.M.J. English Medium School was established in 1988 opposite to Children’s Park, Kurnool. Seeing the need of the hour, the Sisters of JMJ restarted the English Medium school, which had previously been closed in 1969. In the beginning, the school ran in a simple shed, purely for girls. As the years passed, due to circumstances, the management began to admit boys too.

J.M.J. English Medium School is a Catholic school. At that period, the students of our school were the first generation of learners in their families. Their parents belong to the working class (daily laborers, domestic helpers, auto drivers, construction laborers). Our school aims at imparting sound academic excellence while providing opportunities for an integral personality development of students in the light of their key role in the society today. The policy of the management is to admit not only Catholic and Christian youth but also students of all creeds and communities, with the primary concern of promoting education especially among the economically backward classes.

Milestones

Our Journey & Growth

1988

The Humble Beginning

School established opposite to Children’s Park. Initially run in a shed, purely started for girls, laying the foundation for first-generation learners.

1995 - 1996

Building Shift & First SSC Batch

Classes shifted to the Junior College building (the present building). The first-ever batch of students appeared for the SSC Board examinations.

2000

Digital Literacy

Computer laboratory was successfully set up to introduce modern IT skills to our students.

2002

Lab Renovation & Sports Uniform

Science laboratory renovated. KG classrooms painted with colorful pictures for better learning. Sports uniform introduced to foster sportsmanship.

2010

T-Shirts & School Drum Band

T-shirts introduced to students for active physical education, and the school drum band was inaugurated.

2011

Kindergarten Park

KG Park established with play items including slider, see-saw, and nursery merry-go-round for physical play-based learning.

2012

Infrastructure Boost & Tech

Construction of staircase, new staffroom on 2nd floor, drinking water plant, separate toilets for boys/girls. Introduced ILM for English skills and digital classrooms.

2013

Lunch Shed Renovation

Renovated the shed area to provide a comfortable, clean space for students to take their lunch daily.

2017

CCTV & Campus Safety

Installation of CCTV cameras across the campus to ensure safety, discipline, and secure supervision.
